The Saltwater CrownChapter 1 · Northhaven

Iris Vale arrived in Northhaven before sunrise, carrying nothing but a folded map and a salt-stained coat.

Mara Vey watched her from the harbor wall. Her left eye was green, and a pale scar crossed her eyebrow. She kept one hand on the chart case at her side.

Elian Vey had always been quick to trust a stranger. Mara preferred to know where they had sailed from, and what they had left behind.
