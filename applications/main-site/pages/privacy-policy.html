Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Last updated: November 2, 2026

Alysum Writing (“Alysum,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects the privacy of its readers, authors, and community members.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Alysum collects and processes, how that information is used, when it may be shared, how it is protected and retained, and what privacy choices are available to users.

This Policy applies to the Alysum Writing website and its associated account, writing, publishing, reading, community, and digital-access features.

1. Information We Collect

The information Alysum processes depends on the features a user chooses to access.

Account information may include email addresses, usernames, display names, profile pictures, account identifiers, login-related information, and account preferences.

Creative and uploaded content may include manuscripts, chapters, unpublished drafts, writing notes, worldbuilding information, images, and other files users save or submit.

Reading and publishing information may include reading progress, saved works, publishing status, content visibility settings, and interactions with reading or writing features.

Community information may include comments, reviews, reports, and information voluntarily shared through public or collaborative features.

Purchase-related information may include Purchase Pass identifiers, redemption status, product identifiers, transaction references, and information necessary to verify or provide purchased access.

Technical information may include IP addresses, browser and device information, authentication session data, website request logs, and error information processed by Alysum or its infrastructure providers.

Support information may include messages, email addresses, and other details users voluntarily provide when requesting assistance.

Alysum does not necessarily collect every category of information from every user.

2. How Information Is Collected

Information may be obtained:

  • Directly from users when they register, update profiles, upload content, publish works, or contact support.
  • Through normal website operation when users sign in, save content, read works, or use available features.
  • From authentication providers when users access their accounts.
  • From payment or purchase integrations when information is needed to verify purchased access.
  • From hosting and infrastructure services that generate technical logs or security information.
  • From other users when they submit comments, reports, or collaborative content.

Alysum aims to collect and process information relevant to providing and maintaining its services.

3. How We Use Information

Alysum uses personal information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and maintain accounts.
  • Save and retrieve writing projects.
  • Provide private draft storage and publishing features.
  • Display works according to selected visibility and access settings.
  • Maintain reading progress and personal libraries.
  • Operate comments and other community features.
  • Verify and fulfill Purchase Pass access.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain website functionality and security.
  • Investigate technical errors, fraud, or unauthorized access.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Alysum does not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis.

Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis for processing, that basis may include providing requested services, complying with legal obligations, legitimate operational interests, or obtaining consent when required.

4. Private Manuscripts and User Content

Alysum processes manuscripts, drafts, and uploaded materials to provide its writing and publishing functions.

Content saved as private is intended to remain unavailable to the public through ordinary website features.

Alysum may technically process stored content to save, retrieve, synchronize, back up, or display it according to the user's permissions.

Access to private information may be necessary in limited circumstances, including authorized technical support, security investigations, legal compliance, or maintenance.

Alysum does not intentionally publish private manuscripts or make them publicly accessible without authorization, except where disclosure is legally required.

Users should review visibility settings before publishing or sharing content.

Private content may remain temporarily in technical backups after deletion from active systems.

5. Generative AI and Personal Information

Alysum Writing does not use generative artificial intelligence to operate its writing, publishing, moderation, or website functions.

Alysum does not use private manuscripts, published works, personal information, or uploaded creative materials to train or fine-tune generative AI models.

Alysum does not sell or license authors' creative works to AI companies for model training.

Alysum does not intentionally submit private manuscripts to generative AI services for content analysis, rewriting, moderation, or other automated processing.

These commitments concern Alysum's own operations and authorized processing. They do not guarantee the behavior of independent third parties outside Alysum's control.

6. Cookies, Browser Storage, and Technical Logs

Alysum may use cookies, local storage, and similar browser technologies to maintain login sessions, remember preferences, and support essential website functionality.

Hosting and infrastructure providers may automatically process technical information necessary to deliver the website, identify errors, protect against abuse, and maintain system reliability.

This information may include IP addresses, browser characteristics, device information, timestamps, and website request records.

Alysum does not represent that it uses Google Analytics or another third-party visitor-tracking service unless such a service is actually enabled.

If optional analytics, advertising trackers, or similar technologies are introduced, Alysum will update this Policy and provide any notices or consent mechanisms required by applicable law.

Users may control certain cookies and stored information through their browser settings. Disabling essential storage may interfere with account access or website functionality.

7. Purchase Passes and Payment Information

Purchase Passes allow readers to access eligible paid digital content.

Payments may be handled through independent services such as Ko-fi and their associated payment processors.

Depending on the integration, Alysum may process limited purchase information necessary to verify a transaction, register an entitlement, redeem an access code, or resolve an access problem.

Such information may include purchase references, product identifiers, transaction status, and redemption records.

Payment providers independently collect and process information required for payment transactions, including any billing or payment-card information they request.

Alysum does not require users to provide full payment-card numbers or account passwords to its support team.

Authors acting as sellers may receive customer information through their payment providers for order fulfillment, customer support, refunds, or disputes.

Purchase-related information may be retained where necessary to preserve purchased access, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or satisfy legal requirements.

8. When Information May Be Shared

Alysum does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that support website operations, including:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Authentication services.
  • Database and file-storage providers.
  • Email-delivery services.
  • Payment and purchase-related integrations.
  • Security and technical-support services.

These providers may process information needed to deliver their services.

Alysum may disclose information when legally required, in response to valid legal process, or where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect security, or address serious threats.

Information users voluntarily publish or share may be visible to others according to the relevant feature and visibility settings.

Alysum does not authorize service providers to use authors' manuscripts for generative AI training on Alysum's behalf.

9. Data Security

Alysum uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect personal information and stored creative content.

Depending on the systems involved, these safeguards may include authentication controls, access restrictions, secure connections, and protections provided by infrastructure services.

Access to personal information should be limited to legitimate operational needs.

No website, transmission method, or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

If a security incident involving personal information occurs, Alysum will investigate and provide notifications where required by applicable law.

Users who believe their accounts or information have been compromised should contact support.

10. Data Retention and Washington State Law

Alysum retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, and comply with applicable Washington State and federal laws.

Retention periods depend on the type of information and the reason it is processed.

Account information: Generally retained while the account is active and afterward only where necessary for legitimate operational, security, or legal purposes.

Private manuscripts and uploaded content: Generally retained while stored in the user's account, subject to deletion actions, backup processes, and applicable legal obligations.

Purchase and redemption records: Retained as needed to verify access, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable transaction or recordkeeping requirements.

Security and technical logs: Retained for periods reasonably necessary to protect the Service, investigate incidents, and troubleshoot technical problems.

Support communications: Retained as necessary to address requests, document resolutions, and comply with applicable obligations.

Alysum does not retain personal information indefinitely without a legitimate purpose.

Washington State law does not impose one universal retention period for every category of website information. Where a specific state or federal retention requirement applies, Alysum will comply with it.

Where no specific period is legally required, Alysum will determine retention according to the information's purpose, operational necessity, and applicable privacy obligations.

Information deleted from active systems may remain temporarily in backups or records subject to lawful retention requirements.

11. Access, Correction, and Deletion

Users may manage certain personal information through available account settings.

Users may contact Alysum to request:

  • Access to personal information associated with their accounts.
  • Correction of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Information about how their personal data is processed.
  • Assistance with other privacy rights available under applicable law.

Alysum may require reasonable identity verification before completing requests involving sensitive account information.

Some information may need to be retained for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, transaction disputes, or other lawful purposes.

Requests will be reviewed and addressed within applicable legal timeframes.

12. Children's and Teenagers' Privacy

Alysum Writing is intended for users aged 13 or older.

Users aged 13–17 may use eligible features, and providing those features may require processing limited account, authentication, creative-content, and technical information.

Alysum aims to minimize the personal information processed about minors and avoid collecting information that is unnecessary for the features they use.

Alysum does not knowingly permit children under 13 to register accounts.

If Alysum learns that personal information was collected from a child under 13 without any legally required parental consent, appropriate steps will be taken to address the account and delete information where required.

Alysum does not intentionally use minors' personal information for generative AI training.

Parents or legal guardians may contact Alysum regarding a minor's information and exercise applicable privacy rights where legally authorized.

Teenage users are encouraged not to publicly share sensitive personal details, including home addresses, personal phone numbers, school schedules, or financial information.

13. International Data Processing

Alysum's hosting, authentication, storage, or other service providers may process information in countries or regions different from a user's location.

Privacy and data-protection laws may differ between jurisdictions.

Where legally required, Alysum will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal information.

14. Third-Party Websites and Services

Alysum may link to or integrate with third-party websites and services.

These providers may collect or process information independently under their own privacy policies.

Alysum does not control the independent privacy practices of third-party websites.

Users should review the privacy policies of relevant third-party providers, particularly when completing payments or using external authentication services.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Alysum may update this Privacy Policy when its features, technology, service providers, legal obligations, or information-handling practices change.

Updated versions will identify their effective date and last-updated date.

Where required by applicable law, Alysum will provide additional notice or obtain consent before implementing material changes.
