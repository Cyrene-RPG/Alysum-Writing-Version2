Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 13, 2026

1. What We Collect

Alysum collects the information needed to create and operate your account, such as your email address, username, profile details, account type, authentication provider, and the content you create or upload in the service.

2. Content You Create

Your drafts, books, notes, worldbuilding material, comments, and reading activity may be stored so Alysum can provide writing, publishing, library, and feedback features.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create accounts, authenticate users, and keep sessions secure.
  • To save and display your projects, reading activity, settings, and profile data.
  • To provide support, troubleshoot issues, and improve Alysum.
  • To measure basic product usage and performance.

4. Third-Party Services

Alysum may use trusted providers for authentication, hosting, analytics, storage, and database services. These providers process information only as needed to support Alysum's features.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Content may be visible to others when you choose to publish, share, comment, review, or otherwise make it available through Alysum features.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect account and content data. No online service can guarantee perfect security, so keep your account credentials private.

7. Your Choices

You can update account information inside Alysum where settings are available. For help with account data, access, deletion, or privacy questions, contact support.

8. Children's Privacy

Alysum is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as Alysum changes. The effective date above shows when this version was last updated.

10. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to alysum.support@gmail.com.
