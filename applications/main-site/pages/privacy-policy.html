Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Last updated: November 2, 2026

1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Alysum Writing (“Alysum,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, discloses, and protects information when you use our website, accounts, writing and reading tools, publishing features, Purchase Passes, and related services (the “Service”). It should be read together with our Terms of Service.

This Policy covers information handled by Alysum. Independent services, including payment providers, may have their own privacy practices and policies.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect your email address, username, display name, profile details, account type, account identifiers, authentication-provider details, settings, and communications you send to support.

We also process information you choose to create or provide, including drafts, stories, chapters, notes, worldbuilding materials, uploaded files, comments, reviews, and other contributions.

Use of reading, library, publishing, and Purchase Pass features may generate activity records, such as saved books, reading progress, purchase-access status, redemption events, and troubleshooting records. We may receive technical information needed for security and operation, such as device or browser details, IP addresses, request logs, and error information.

3. Writing, Drafts, and Visibility

Your creative works remain yours, subject to third-party rights and our Terms of Service. Alysum processes and stores content to provide writing, synchronization, publishing, library, collaboration, and related features.

Private drafts are intended to remain private through ordinary Service features. Content you publish, share, submit to a collaboration feature, or post as a comment or review may be visible to other users according to the feature and settings you select. Do not include information you do not want others to see in public content.

Authorized personnel or service providers may need limited access to stored information for support, security, abuse investigations, legal compliance, or technical maintenance. We do not promise that online storage is perfectly secure; please maintain your own backups.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to create and authenticate accounts, maintain sessions, save and synchronize content, provide reading and publishing tools, manage visibility and access settings, register or redeem Purchase Passes, respond to support requests, prevent fraud and abuse, troubleshoot problems, maintain security, and improve reliability and performance.

We may use aggregate or operational usage information to understand which features are working and where technical improvements are needed. We do not use creative works for generative AI training.

5. No Generative AI and AI Training

Alysum does not use generative AI to operate its writing, publishing, moderation, or analytics features. Alysum does not submit authors' creative works to generative AI services for writing assistance, moderation, analytics, or model training, and does not authorize its service providers to train generative AI models on those works on Alysum's behalf.

Alysum does not sell or license authors' manuscripts for AI model training. Our Terms of Service separately prohibit users from uploading AI-generated creative content and restrict unauthorized collection of others' works for AI training.

This statement describes Alysum's own practices and instructions to its providers; it does not purport to control independent actions by third parties outside Alysum's Service.

6. Accounts, Authentication, and Security

Account and authentication information is used to verify identity, manage sessions, protect accounts, and investigate suspicious activity. Authentication providers may separately handle login information under their own policies.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the Service. No system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your credentials private and contact support if you suspect unauthorized access.

7. Purchase Passes and Payments

Purchase Passes may involve independent payment services such as Ko-fi and their payment processors. Alysum may receive limited transaction or order information needed to identify a purchased product, confirm payment status, issue or redeem access codes, prevent duplicate fulfillment, resolve access issues, and provide support.

Payment providers independently determine what financial information they collect and retain under their own policies. Alysum does not require you to send passwords or full payment-card details to support. Authors acting as sellers may receive information necessary to fulfill orders and handle refunds or disputes under the payment provider's processes.

Transaction and redemption records may be retained where reasonably necessary for access verification, fraud prevention, disputes, accounting, or legal obligations.

8. Analytics, Cookies, and Similar Technologies

Alysum may use essential browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies to keep users signed in, remember settings, and operate site features. The website also loads an analytics integration to measure basic usage and performance.

Analytics and hosting providers may process technical information such as device characteristics, pages visited, approximate location derived from network information, and request metadata, depending on their configuration. Any optional analytics or tracking that legally requires consent must be handled according to applicable law. Browser controls may allow you to manage cookies, though blocking essential storage can affect functionality.

9. When Information Is Shared

We may share information with service providers that support authentication, hosting, databases, storage, analytics, email delivery, security, and payment-related functionality, only as reasonably needed for those services and subject to applicable agreements and law.

Information you intentionally publish or share may be accessible to readers, collaborators, or the public as indicated by the relevant feature. We may also disclose information when legally required, to protect rights or safety, to investigate fraud or abuse, or as part of a lawful business transition with appropriate safeguards.

Alysum does not sell users' personal information. We do not share authors' creative works with generative AI services for training.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the Service, maintain accounts and purchased access, resolve disputes, secure systems, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods vary by record type and operational requirements.

When you delete content or request account deletion, information may not disappear immediately from backups, security logs, transaction records, or records that must be retained by law. We will review deletion requests and remove or de-identify eligible information as reasonably practicable, subject to lawful exceptions.

Removing an account or paid work does not automatically eliminate refund, fulfillment, or other obligations arising from past transactions.

11. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You may update certain profile information, settings, and content through available account features. You may contact support to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to ask questions about how information is processed.

We may need to verify the requester's identity before releasing or changing information. Rights and exceptions vary by jurisdiction; we will handle requests in accordance with applicable law. Where required, we will provide applicable appeal or complaint information.

12. Children and Teen Users

Alysum accounts are intended for users aged 13 or older. Users aged 13–17 may use eligible features subject to applicable law and the Terms of Service, but must be at least 18 to sell content, create paid Purchase Passes, or receive author payments.

We do not knowingly permit children under 13 to register. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deletion where required. Parents or guardians may contact support about legally authorized privacy requests.

Teen users should avoid posting personal contact information, addresses, or other sensitive details publicly.

13. International Use and Data Processing

Service providers and infrastructure may process or store information in locations different from where a user lives. Privacy protections and legal requirements may differ by jurisdiction. Where applicable law requires safeguards for transfers, we will use appropriate measures.

14. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our features, service providers, data practices, or legal obligations change. We will update the effective and last-updated dates and provide additional notice of material changes when appropriate or legally required.

15. Contact

For privacy questions, requests to access or delete information, concerns about a minor's data, or suspected misuse of information, contact alysum.support@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card details, or unnecessary sensitive information.
