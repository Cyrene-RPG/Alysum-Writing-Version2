Try it safely first

Don’t test restore on your main account until you’re comfortable. Use the practice flow below — it never reads or overwrites your real books.

  1. Download practice backup — a ZIP with two fake books.
  2. Preview restore — see exactly what would change. Nothing is saved.
  3. When you trust it, restore the practice ZIP in local guest mode (desktop app → Host Local), or use your real backup on your account.

1Create backup

Packages your books as HTML pages inside a ZIP file. The filename includes today’s date and time on your computer. Nothing is uploaded to Alysum’s servers.

2Restore backup

Select a ZIP you saved earlier and load it back into Alysum. Preview restore first if this is your real account. Make a new backup before overwriting anything.
