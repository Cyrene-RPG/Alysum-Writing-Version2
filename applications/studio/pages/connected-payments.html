What works automatically?

One-time Ko-fi Shop and Payhip product purchases. Each product can unlock selected chapters or every chapter of a finished book.

Readers must use their verified Alysum email at checkout. Tips, gifts, memberships and other services are not connected for automatic access yet. Keep using a purchase link for those services.
