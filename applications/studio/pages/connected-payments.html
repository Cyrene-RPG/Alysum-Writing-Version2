Sell through your own page

Use your own shop or membership service for payments and paid content.

Add a purchase link

Open a book in Studio, choose Publish, and add your shop or membership address under “Sell through your own page”.

Readers will see “Buy from author” on your book page. Only select chapters you want to make free on Alysum.

Purchases happen on your linked service and do not unlock chapters on Alysum. There is no Stripe connection or payment setup required here.
