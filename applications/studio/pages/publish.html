How purchases unlock chapters

Use one-time shop products. A shop product can contain one chapter or a bundle. In mixed mode, choose its chapters on its product card. Tips, gifts and memberships do not unlock chapters.

Readers use the same email as their verified Alysum account at checkout. A confirmed purchase unlocks only its included chapters at that time.
