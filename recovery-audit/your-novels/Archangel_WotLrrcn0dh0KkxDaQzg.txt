My body dropped sideways in a sharp dodge before I’d even fully registered the danger, servos screaming in protest as the motion forced my armor to pivot faster than my limiters would normally have allowed. Angles, timing, survival odds, but they didn’t sound like anything I needed to hear. I let most of it fall away. The world narrowed. Footsteps on concrete. Weight shifts.
